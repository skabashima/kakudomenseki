# ストア掲載情報(英語版)― Zukei Hunter

英語版のアプリ名は **Zukei Hunter**(アプリ内・ホーム画面の表示名も同じ。
`project.godot` の `config/name_localized` と `i18n/en.json` の「図形ハンター」で決めている)。
日本語版の `ストア掲載情報.md` と同じ構成で、そのまま貼れる形にしてある。
字数は英語でも各ストアの上限(文字数)がそのまま当たる。

**ストア側でやること(アプリとは別)**
- Google Play Console: 「ストアの設定 → ストアの掲載情報 → 翻訳を追加」で **English (United States)** を足し、下の文と `store/入稿セット_en/` の画像を入れる。既定の言語は日本語のまま。
- App Store Connect: アプリの「ローカリゼーションを追加」で **English (U.S.)** を足し、下の文と `store/入稿セット_en/` の画像を入れる。プライマリ言語は日本語のまま(.ipa の `CFBundleLocalizations` に ja と en が入るので、言語欄に両方出る)。
- App内課金の表示名・説明も、両ストアの英語ローカリゼーションに入れる(§4)。
- 価格はストアの価格帯で自動換算される(¥500 → 各国の相当額)。文中には値段を書かない(審査で弾かれる)。

### 詳細な説明(4000字以内)

```
■ Geometry from elementary school to high school, in one app

Zukei Hunter is a learning game built only around angles and area: from
elementary geometry (plane shapes and solids) to high school trigonometry,
vectors and integrals, in 65 stages.

■ Three stories, one for each age

- Kids: "Treasure Map", 23 steps. Follow one map from the beach to the
  treasure chest. Each unit is one step, and every step is hands-on: tear off
  the corners of a triangle and line them up, roll a circle and count the
  diameters, pour water and watch the depth. You check the rule with your
  fingers before you read the formula.
- Middle school: "The Measuring Journey", 21 chapters. A surveyor's field book
  of a land to measure. Move a figure, find what does not change, and test the
  claims of the Crow, a wandering scholar who sells formulas one town ahead.
- High school: "Orbit Lab", 9 chapters. On a newly settled planet, fix landing
  sites, relay towers and launch trajectories with mathematics alone: power of
  a point, the law of cosines, the law of sines, radians, circles, integrals.

Story answers use the same calculator as the main game - no multiple choice.

■ Answers can't be memorized

Every problem is generated on the spot: 30,832 distinct question-and-answer
pairs, so nothing can be memorized.

■ See the figure. Type the number.

A stage is 3 questions in a row with 3 hearts. A miss costs a heart; at zero
you retry with new numbers. The hearts you keep become your stars.

Angles (20 stages): triangles, parallel lines, isosceles triangles, clock
hands, polygons, boomerangs, folds, exterior angles, star angles, inscribed
angles, tangents, constructions, triangle centers, trigonometric equations,
angles between vectors, lines and planes.

Area (45 stages): rectangles, triangles, quadrilaterals, circles and sectors,
roads through a field, composite shapes, the leaf, volume and surface area,
nets and dice, tanks, moving shapes, similarity, cutting solids, Pythagoras,
special right triangles, coordinate geometry, the lunes of Hippocrates,
pyramids and cones, Euler's formula, power of a point, Ceva and Menelaus,
the laws of sines and cosines, Heron's formula, vectors, radians, the
equation of a circle, optimization, parabolas (the 1/6 formula), solids of
revolution.

Each course runs from elementary to high school level; clearing a stage
unlocks the next.

■ The Gauntlet: the method changes, not just the numbers

Every cleared stage gets a 10-question Gauntlet. The difficulty rises each
question, and so does the method - around 245 methods in all. Clear all 10
for the stage's crown.

■ Net Master: nets fold into solids on the spot

Which solid does this net make? Right or wrong, you watch the net fold up
into the solid. 101 nets: cubes, boxes, prisms, pyramids, the tetrahedron and
octahedron, cylinders and cones - 11 different nets for the cube alone.
Unlocking adds a random 10-net Gauntlet and a 10-question Practice run.

■ Calculator built in

Type an expression, not just a number: × ÷ + − ( ) √ are accepted, and
"= Calc" works out partial results. A malformed expression costs no heart.

■ Stuck? Watch the solution step by step

Each tap advances the solution one step: helper lines are drawn, the key part
is highlighted, equal angles are marked.

■ Reasons to keep going

Ranks from Apprentice to Geometry Sage, combos that boost your score, a
3-minute Time Attack, a 3-miss Survival mode, Island Grab (race the Crow for
territory over 24 islands), a Daily Shape, and stars and bests for every stage.

■ Safe to use

No ads. No network use apart from the purchase itself. No personal data
collected. One-time purchase, no subscriptions.

■ What's free

The first 4 stages of each course, Time Attack (Angles), the first steps of
each story, 2 islands per range, the first 8 Net Master puzzles, and the
calculator, solutions, helper lines, hints, ranks and records.
One purchase unlocks the remaining 57 stages, the rest of the stories and
islands, all 101 nets with the Net Master runs, every Gauntlet and all of
Challenge.
```

## 5. 英語の画像の作り方

```

python store/make_store_images.py --en                   # 見出しを合成 → store/入稿セット_en
python store/make_app_store_creative.py --en             # App Store のヘッダと検索結果の画像 → 入稿セット_en/app_store_creative
python store/check_store_images.py --en                  # 寸法と枚数の検品
```

## 6. 注意

- 英語の文は `python store/check_store_text.py` で字数と使えない字を見ている(このファイルも対象)。
- 「free」はアプリ名・キーワードに入れない(審査で弾かれる)。
- 学年の呼び方: 中学受験レベル = Elementary、高校受験レベル = Middle school、大学受験レベル = High school(アプリ内と同じ)。
